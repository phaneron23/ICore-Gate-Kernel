## Verification Protocol

Each component is tested against six questions:

| # | Question | What It Proves |
|---|----------|---------------|
| 1 | Does it exist? | Ontological status — the component is real, not hypothetical |
| 2 | Can it be uniquely identified? | Identity — it can be distinguished from all other components |
| 3 | What relationships define it? | Structural position — its dependencies and dependents are explicit |
| 4 | What constraints govern it? | Governance — the rules that limit its behavior are defined |
| 5 | What transformations are permitted? | Evolution — how it can change without breaking the system |
| 6 | How can its correctness be verified? | Proof — independent verification is possible |
