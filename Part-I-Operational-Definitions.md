# ICore.Gate — Minimal Constitutional Kernel v1.0

## Part I — Operational Definitions

Every abstraction answers exactly four questions. Nothing more.
Definitions follow derivation order: each builds on the one before it.

---

### USCP — Universal Sovereign Core Primitives

**What is it?** The smallest complete set of constitutional primitives — six irreducible atoms from which every constitutional system is derived:

| # | Primitive | Question It Answers |
|---|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Existence** | What is? |
| 2 | **Identity** | What distinguishes it? |
| 3 | **Relationship** | How is it connected? |
| 4 | **Constraint** | What governs or limits it? |
| 5 | **Transformation** | How does it change? |
| 6 | **Verification** | How do we know it is valid? |

**Why does it exist?** To anchor every constitutional claim in something that cannot be further decomposed. These six are sufficient because every constitutional system must answer all six questions — nothing more is needed, nothing is missing.

**What does it depend on?** Reality and Principles — the two pre-constitutional layers that USCP translates into constitutional form.

**What depends on it?** USC — the Constitution cannot be organized from what has not first been reduced to primitives.

---

### USC — Universal Sovereign Core (The Constitution)

**What is it?** The complete Constitution — the sovereign constitutional nucleus that organizes USCP into a coherent, self-governing, authoritative foundation. USC is not *part of* the Constitution; USC *is* the Constitution.

**Why does it exist?** To provide a single, indivisible constitutional authority from which all derivative systems inherit legitimacy. Everything below USC is pre-constitutional (Reality, Principles, USCP). Everything above USC is constitutionally derived (UCE through Implementations).

**What does it depend on?** USCP — a Constitution without primitives is opinion; primitives without a Constitution are noise.

**What depends on it?** UCE, UCC, UCM, UCL, UCRS, and transitively everything above — every epistemological, logical, mathematical, linguistic, and structural claim flows from the Constitution.

---

### UCE — Universal Constitutional Epistemology

**What is it?** The constitutional science of knowledge — the discipline within USC that governs what can be known, how truth is verified, and how knowledge is derived.

**Why does it exist?** To ensure that all knowledge claims made in the name of ICore are constitutionally grounded, verifiable, and non-contradictory.

**What does it depend on?** USC — epistemology without the Constitution has no authority to declare what is true.

**What depends on it?** UCC — the calculus cannot reason about what epistemology has not first classified as knowable.

---

### UCC — Universal Constitutional Calculus

**What is it?** The constitutional science of reasoning — the discipline within USC that governs how constitutional truths are derived, combined, and validated through formal logic.

**Why does it exist?** To provide formal, verifiable reasoning over constitutional knowledge — ensuring every derivation is traceable to its source.

**What does it depend on?** UCE — calculus without epistemology manipulates symbols without meaning.

**What depends on it?** UCM — mathematics requires a logical substrate to ground its formal structures.

---

### UCM — Universal Constitutional Mathematics

**What is it?** The constitutional science of structure — the discipline within USC that provides formal mathematical structures for representation, transformation, and computation over constitutional objects.

**Why does it exist?** To ground constitutional operations in mathematical rigor, enabling precise representation without ambiguity.

**What does it depend on?** UCC — mathematics without constitutional logic is abstract algebra; with it, it is constitutional algebra.

**What depends on it?** UCL — a language requires mathematical structure to express meaning without loss.

---

### UCL — Universal Constitutional Language

**What is it?** The constitutional science of expression — the canonical language for expressing constitutional meaning. The single semantic substrate from which all representations are derived.

**Why does it exist?** To separate meaning from serialization: meaning is constitutional; JSON-LD, CBOR-LD, and every other format are replaceable implementations.

**What does it depend on?** UCM — a language without mathematical structure cannot guarantee semantic precision.

**What depends on it?** UCRS and UCModels — reference systems and canonical models are expressed in UCL, not in any serialization format.

---

### UCRS — Universal Constitutional Reference System

**What is it?** The canonical reference that represents the Constitution — the system that provides reference points and spatial orientation within constitutional space.

**Why does it exist?** To ensure every constitutional entity can be uniquely located, related, and navigated without ambiguity. UCRS is the Constitution's self-representation.

**What does it depend on?** UCL — references must be expressed in the canonical language to remain meaningful across implementations.

**What depends on it?** UCModels — models need reference coordinates to bind entities to their constitutional positions.

---

### UCModels — Universal Canonical Models

**What is it?** The set of canonical models that represent constitutional entities, their relationships, and their lifecycle processes.

**Why does it exist?** To provide shared, verifiable models of constitutional reality that all downstream systems consume — the ontological backbone.

**What does it depend on?** UCL and UCRS — models are expressed in UCL and oriented by UCRS.

**What depends on it?** URS — the representation system maps canonical models into accessible forms.

---

### URS — Universal Representation System

**What is it?** The system that maps constitutional meaning (from UCModels) into representational forms — textual, structural, and visual.

**Why does it exist?** To make constitutional knowledge accessible and communicable to humans and machines without altering its meaning.

**What does it depend on?** UCModels — representation without canonical models is formatting without substance.

**What depends on it?** UVS — visualization is a specialized representation pathway.

---

### UVS — Universal Visualization System

**What is it?** The system that renders constitutional knowledge into human-interpretable, interactive visual forms — enabling narrative-driven understanding.

**Why does it exist?** To fulfill the constitutional engineering mandate of interactive, narrative-driven understanding of Origin, Nature, Structure, and Culture.

**What does it depend on?** URS — visualization is a representation; it cannot render what has not been represented.

**What depends on it?** USR/CoreFab — the execution engine consumes visual and interactive constitutional interfaces.

---

### USR / CoreFab — Universal Constitutional Execution Engine / Runtime

**What is it?** The constitutional runtime that executes constitutional operations with sovereignty guarantees — the engine where constitutional logic becomes constitutional action.

**Why does it exist?** To ensure that constitutional operations execute with verified provenance, sovereign control, and minimal resource consumption.

**What does it depend on?** UVS, and transitively the entire stack above USCP — execution requires the full constitutional foundation to be meaningful.

**What depends on it?** UCA — adapters bridge the execution engine to external systems.

---

### UCA — Universal Constitutional Adapter

**What is it?** The universal adapter that maps constitutional interfaces to external systems — naming (DNS, ENS, Handshake), storage, execution (WASM, Rust, Deno), and any future infrastructure.

**Why does it exist?** To maintain constitutional sovereignty while interfacing with any external system: the constitution never depends on an adapter, but adapters depend on the constitution.

**What does it depend on?** USR/CoreFab — adapters bridge the runtime, not raw abstractions.

**What depends on it?** UCD and all external integrations — derivatives and implementations access the outside world through UCA.

---

### UCD — Universal Constitutional Derivatives

**What is it?** The set of constitutionally-derived building blocks, services, and subsystems that implement specific capabilities while remaining constitutionally compliant.

**Why does it exist?** To provide ready-made, verified constitutional components that implementations can compose without re-deriving from first principles.

**What does it depend on?** UCA — derivatives adapt constitutional capability to specific domains.

**What depends on it?** Implementations — applications compose derivatives into concrete systems.

---

### CodeLabs

**What is it?** The sandboxed constitutional experimentation environment where new ideas are prototyped, validated, and refined before they enter the stable kernel.

**Why does it exist?** To provide a safe proving ground that prevents unverified ideas from contaminating the constitutional foundation — ICore.Gate's primary function.

**What does it depend on?** The full constitutional stack (via UCA) — experimentation requires the complete foundation to be meaningful.

**What depends on it?** Studio — the unified workspace integrates CodeLabs as its interactive experimentation surface.

---

### Studio (studio.initialcore.net)

**What is it?** The unified constitutional workspace that integrates CodeLabs, the Constitutional Atlas, and all constitutional tools into one sovereign, accessible interface.

**Why does it exist?** To provide a single entry point for all constitutional work — governed, verified, visualized, and executed in one place.

**What does it depend on?** CodeLabs and the full constitutional stack — the workspace is the outermost interface, consuming everything beneath it.

**What depends on it?** Nothing within the kernel — Studio is the outermost layer. External users and systems interact with ICore through Studio.

---

### Architecture Summary

```
Pre-constitutional:    Reality → Principles
Constitutional base:   USCP (6 primitives) → USC (the Constitution)
Constitutional sciences: UCE → UCC → UCM → UCL
Constitutional reference: UCRS → UCModels
Constitutional expression: URS → UVS
Constitutional execution: USR/CoreFab → UCA
Constitutional derivatives: UCD → Implementations
Interface layer:       CodeLabs → Studio
```

*Every definition above is constitutional. The derivation graph (Part II) makes the dependency order explicit.*
