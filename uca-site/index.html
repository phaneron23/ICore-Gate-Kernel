Adapter Axioms

Four non-negotiable principles that govern all adapters.

AA1
Constitution Never Depends on Adapter

The direction of dependency is absolute: adapters depend on the constitution. The constitution never depends on an adapter. Remove every adapter — the constitution still stands.

AA2
Adapters Are Replaceable

Any adapter may be replaced by any other adapter fulfilling the same constitutional purpose, without constitutional change. Swap DNS for ENS — the constitution doesn't notice.

AA3
The Boundary Is Absolute

UCA is the last constitutional layer. Everything beyond UCA is external. No external system has constitutional standing. The boundary is the constitution's edge.

AA4
Constitutional Only When Bridging

An adapter exists only to bridge a specific constitutional capability to a specific external system. No bridge, no adapter. No constitutional purpose, no adapter.
