## Platform Strategy

**PWA-first • Mobile/Android-first • Offline-first • Lightweight • Portable • Downloadable • Sovereign • Cross-platform**

Every prototype, domain, and subdomain (including initialcore.net and icore.gate) should function as a self-contained constitutional artifact that is:

- Instantly viewable.
- Installable as a PWA.
- Offline-capable.
- Lightweight enough for constrained devices.
- Portable across Android, Linux, Windows, macOS, and the web.
- Human-reviewable and replaceable.

This aligns with ICore's doctrine: sovereignty, simplicity, capability before capacity, necessity before scaling.
