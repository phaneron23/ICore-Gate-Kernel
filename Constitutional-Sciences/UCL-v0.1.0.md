## Section 3: Expression Rules

### 3.1 The Expression Structure

Every constitutional expression must contain:

| Field | Requirement | Derived From |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Subject** | The constitutional object being expressed. | Identity |
| **Predicate** | The relationship or property being asserted. | Relationship |
| **Object** | The value, referent, or scope of the assertion. | Constraint |
| **Source** | The derivation chain backing the expression. | Verification (UCE) |
| **Context** | The epistemic state (Knowledge, Assertion, Ignorance). | Verification (UCE) |

### 3.2 Expression Properties

| # | Property | Statement |
|---|----------|-----------|
| EP1 | **Unambiguity.** | Every expression must have exactly one interpretation within the constitutional stack. |
| EP2 | **Compositionality.** | The meaning of a complex expression is determined by the meanings of its parts and the rules for combining them. |
| EP3 | **Context-independence.** | The meaning of an expression does not depend on who wrote it or where it appears. |
| EP4 | **Completeness.** | The vocabulary and expression rules must be sufficient to express any constitutional claim. |
| EP5 | **Minimality.** | The vocabulary and expression rules must contain no unnecessary terms or structures. |

### 3.3 Expression Forms

UCL defines three expression forms:

| Form | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| **Declarative** | States a constitutional fact. | "UCE is derived from USC." |
| **Operational** | Prescribes a constitutional process. | "Every claim must pass V0→V3 verification." |
| **Structural** | Describes a constitutional relationship. | "UCE, UCC, UCM → UCL (convergence)." |

All three forms use the same expression structure (Section 3.1). The form determines the communicative intent; the structure determines the formal content.

## Section 4: Semantic Precision

### 4.1 What Semantic Precision Requires

| Requirement | Statement |
|------------|-----------|
| **Definition** | Every term must have a clear, explicit definition. Undefined terms are forbidden. |
| **Distinction** | Every term must be distinguishable from every other term. No two terms may have identical meaning. |
| **Consistency** | The meaning of a term must not change across contexts within a version. |
| **Traceability** | Every term's meaning must be traceable to its derivation source. |

### 4.2 Semantic Verification

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Enumerate all terms used in an expression. |
| 2 | Verify each term has a definition in the canonical vocabulary (Section 2.1) or a governed extension (VR4). |
| 3 | Verify no term is used with a meaning that contradicts its canonical definition. |
| 4 | Verify the expression's compositionality (EP2) — complex meaning follows from parts + rules. |
| 5 | Record the verification. |

### 4.3 Semantic Constraints

| # | Constraint | Statement |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| SC1 | **No undefined terms.** | A term without a constitutional definition cannot appear in a constitutional expression. |
| SC2 | **No silent meaning shift.** | The meaning of a term in an expression must match its canonical definition in the vocabulary. |
| SC3 | **No ambiguity by design.** | When an expression could be read two ways, it must be rewritten for precision. |
| SC4 | **No serialization leakage.** | The meaning of an expression must not depend on its serialization format. Meaning is format-independent. |

## Section 5: Serialization Independence

### 5.1 The Principle

**Meaning is constitutional. Serialization is implementation.**

The constitution defines what is meant. Adapters (UCA) determine how it is encoded. No serialization format has constitutional authority.

### 5.2 Supported Serializations

| Format | Status | Role |
|--------|--------|------|
| **JSON-LD** | Default | Human-readable, web-native, W3C-aligned. Primary format for constitutional data. |
| **CBOR-LD** | Compact | Binary encoding. Used when bandwidth or storage is constrained (Android-first). |
| **Natural Language** | Informal | Human communication. Valid for proposals and reviews, not for the canonical record. |
| **Future formats** | Adaptable | Any superior serialization absorbed through UCA without constitutional change. |

### 5.3 Serialization Rules

| # | Rule |
|---|------|
| SR1 | **Meaning preservation.** A serialization is valid if and only if the meaning of the expression is preserved exactly. |
| SR2 | **Round-trip fidelity.** Serializing → deserializing an expression must yield an expression with identical meaning. |
| SR3 | **Format agnosticism.** No constitutional test may produce different results based on serialization format. |
| SR4 | **Adaptation through UCA.** New formats are added through the adapter layer, not through constitutional change. |
