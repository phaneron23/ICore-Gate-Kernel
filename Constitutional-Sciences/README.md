## The Sciences

| # | Science | Full Name | Depends On | Governs |
|---|---------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **UCE** | Universal Constitutional Epistemology | USC | What can be known, how truth is verified |
| 2 | **UCC** | Universal Constitutional Calculus | USC, UCE | How truths are derived, combined, validated |
| 3 | **UCM** | Universal Constitutional Mathematics | USC, UCC | Formal structures for representation and computation |
| 4 | **UCL** | Universal Constitutional Language | USC, UCE, UCC, UCM | The canonical language for expressing meaning |

## Dependency Graph

```
        USC
         │
    ┌────┼────┐
    ↓    ↓    ↓
   UCE  UCC  UCM
    └────┼────┘
         ↓
        UCL
```

UCE, UCC, and UCM branch from USC and converge at UCL. This reflects that epistemology, reasoning, and structure are parallel disciplines that together produce expression.
