## Section 1: Constitutional Knowledge

### 1.1 Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Constitutional Knowledge** | A claim that has passed UCE's verification protocol and is authorized to enter the constitutional record. |
| **Constitutional Assertion** | A claim that has been made but not yet verified. Assertions may become knowledge or be rejected. |
| **Constitutional Ignorance** | A recognized absence of knowledge — the system's honest acknowledgment of what it does not know. |

### 1.2 The Three Epistemic States

Every claim within ICore exists in exactly one of three states:

```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│  CONSTITUTIONAL          │  ← Passed verification. Enters the record.
│  KNOWLEDGE               │
└─────────────────────────┘

┌─────────────────────────┐
│  CONSTITUTIONAL          │  ← Made but unverified. Awaiting audit.
│  ASSERTION               │
└─────────────────────────┘

┌─────────────────────────┐
│  CONSTITUTIONAL          │  ← Recognized absence. Not a failure — a feature.
│  IGNORANCE               │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

No claim exists outside these three states. The system is never silent about what it knows, what it claims, or what it does not know.

## Section 2: Knowledge Claims

### 2.1 Claim Structure

Every knowledge claim must contain:

| Field | Requirement | Derived From |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| **Statement** | The claim itself, stated precisely and unambiguously. | Identity |
| **Source** | The derivation origin — USCP, USC, a verified higher layer, or empirical evidence. | Relationship |
| **Basis** | The reasoning or evidence supporting the claim. | Verification |
| **Scope** | The domain and limits within which the claim holds. | Constraint |
| **Status** | The epistemic state: Knowledge, Assertion, or Ignorance. | Transformation |

A claim missing any field is structurally incomplete and cannot enter the verification pipeline.

### 2.2 Claim Rules

| Rule | Statement |
|------|-----------|
| **C1: Citation is mandatory.** | Every claim must cite its source. An uncited claim is an orphaned assertion — it cannot enter the constitutional record. |
| **C2: No anonymous knowledge.** | Every claim must be attributable to a derivation chain. Knowledge without provenance is not knowledge. |
| **C3: Claims are versioned.** | When a claim is refined, the previous version is archived, not destroyed. The constitutional record is append-only for knowledge. |
| **C4: Scope is explicit.** | Every claim declares where it applies and where it does not. Unscoped claims are presumptively invalid. |
| **C5: Contradiction is suspension.** | If a new claim contradicts existing verified knowledge, the new claim is held as Assertion until the contradiction is resolved through governance. |

### 2.3 Claim Lifecycle

```
Proposed → Audited → Verified → Published → Active
                                    ↓
                              Superseded (archived, never deleted)
                                    ↓
                              Retired (via governance only)
```

No claim may skip stages. No claim may be deleted from the record. The lifecycle is governed by Part IV.

## Section 5: Knowledge Governance

### 5.1 Knowledge Authority

| Role | Scope | Limit |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Knowledge Proposer** | Any participant may propose knowledge claims. | Cannot self-verify. |
| **Knowledge Reviewer** | Any participant may review claims. | Cannot verify their own review. |
| **Knowledge Verifier** | Any participant may verify claims. | Must be independent of proposer and reviewer. |
| **Knowledge Ratifier** | Constitutional authority approves entry into the record. | Cannot propose or review in the same cycle. |

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Statement | The verified claim. |
| Source | Derivation chain. |
| Basis | Evidence and reasoning. |
| Scope | Domain and limits. |

### 6.1 UCE Conformance Tests

An implementation conforms to UCE if and only if:

| # | Test | Pass Criterion |

| UC5 | **Scope Respect** | No claim exceeds its epistemic horizon. Claims do not overreach their derivation layer. |
| UC6 | **Consistency** | No verified knowledge contradicts other verified knowledge. Contradictions are caught and resolved. |

### 6.2 Conformance Protocol

1. **Apply all 8 tests** to the implementation's knowledge base.
2. **Record pass/fail** with evidence for each test.
3. **Determine result:**
   - **8/8 pass** → UCE-conformant.
   - **7/8 pass** → Conditionally conformant (failed test remediated within one governance cycle).
   - **≤6/8 pass** → Non-conformant (re-derivation required).
