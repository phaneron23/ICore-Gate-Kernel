# Atlas v1.0 — Pre-Constitutional Layer

*What exists before the constitution begins.*

---

## Reality

### 1. Canonical Definition
The raw, uninterpreted fact that something exists — the pre-constitutional substrate independent of any constitutional framework.

### 2. Constitutional Origin
Pre-constitutional. Reality is not derived from the constitution; the constitution is derived from Reality. It is the ground floor of the entire derivation chain.

### 3. Operational Meaning
Reality is what ICore interacts with but does not create. The adapter layer (UCA) is where the constitution meets Reality. Reality always has the final say — no constitutional claim can override a verified physical fact.

### 4. Derivation Path
```
Reality (pre-constitutional, no derivation)
  ↓
Principles (pre-constitutional, grounded in Reality)
  ↓
USCP (constitutional, derived from Principles)
```

### 5. Dependencies
None. Reality is the absolute ground. Nothing precedes it.

### 6. Constraints
- Reality is not part of the constitution
- The constitution does not create Reality
- The constitution governs how we reason about Reality, not what Reality is
- Constitutional claims must not contradict verified Reality

### 7. Verification Criteria
- Any claim referencing Reality can be checked against observed, empirical fact
- Constitutional independence: Reality exists without the constitution

### 8. Governance Status
**Not governable.** Reality is pre-constitutional. It cannot be amended, modified, or removed through governance. It simply is.

### 9. Reference Implementations
- UCA adapters (where constitution meets external reality)
- Physical environment (Android device, sensors, network)

### 10. Cross-References
- [USCP — Existence](./Atlas-02-USCP.md) — the constitutional translation of Reality
- [UCA — Adapter](./Atlas-06-Execution.md) — the boundary where constitution meets Reality

## Principles

### 1. Canonical Definition
Fundamental truths that bridge raw existence and governed structure — the philosophical foundations that make constitutional governance possible.

### 2. Constitutional Origin
Pre-constitutional. Principles are not derived from the constitution; the constitution is derived from Principles. They are the bridge between Reality and USCP.

### 3. Operational Meaning
Principles translate raw existence into constitutional primitives. They answer: "Why these six primitives and not others?" Without Principles, USCP would be arbitrary. With Principles, USCP is inevitable.

### 4. Derivation Path
```
Reality → Principles (pre-constitutional bridge)
              ↓
           USCP (constitutional, derived from Principles)
```

### 5. Dependencies
- Reality (Principles are grounded in what exists)

### 6. Constraints
- Principles are values, not rules — they guide but do not enforce
- Principles are not governable through Part IV (they pre-date governance)
- Every constitutional decision should be consistent with Principles
- Principles cannot contradict Reality

### 7. Verification Criteria
- Consistency with Reality
- Sufficiency: together with Reality, they make USCP derivable
- Non-circularity: Principles do not reference the constitution they ground

### 8. Governance Status
**Not governable.** Principles are pre-constitutional. They can be articulated more precisely (Clarification) but not changed in substance.

- Part II of the Kernel (derivation graph embodying Principles)
