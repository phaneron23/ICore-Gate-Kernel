# Atlas — USC: The Constitution

*Not a layer above the Constitution. USC IS the Constitution.*

---

## Origin

USC arises from the convergence of the six primitives into a coherent, self-governing authority. Before USC, there are six irreducible facts about constitutional systems. After USC, there is a **Constitution** — a single, indivisible source of legitimacy from which everything else derives.

The need for USC is the need for **unity**. Six primitives without a Constitution are six isolated truths. Six primitives *with* a Constitution are a **system** — one that can govern, verify, evolve, and express itself.

---

## Nature

USC is the **sovereign constitutional nucleus**. It is not one thing among many; it is the **one thing** from which all other things derive their legitimacy. In political constitutions, the constitution is the supreme law — every other law derives from it. In ICore, USC is the supreme abstraction — every other abstraction derives from it.

**Essential property:** Indivisibility. USC cannot be split into sub-constitutions without losing its sovereignty. It is one, complete, self-contained.

### What USC Is Not
- **USC is not USCP.** USCP provides the primitives; USC organizes them into authority.
- **USC is not UCE/UCC/UCM/UCL.** These are sciences *within* USC — disciplines that the Constitution governs.
- **USC is not UCRS.** UCRS is the Constitution's *self-representation* — how USC knows where everything is within its own space.
- **USC is not a document.** USC is a *relationship* — the relationship between primitives and authority.

## Culture

USC is the **constitutional center of gravity**. In the ICore ecosystem, every decision, every derivation, every verification traces back to USC. When someone asks "why is this true?", the answer is "because the Constitution says so." When someone asks "why should I trust this?", the answer is "because the Constitution verifies it."

### The Constitution's Promise

USC promises three things to everything above it:
1. **Legitimacy** — every derivation from USC inherits constitutional authority
2. **Stability** — USC does not change lightly; it changes only through governance
3. **Verifiability** — every claim made by USC can be checked against its primitives

And USC promises one thing to everything below it:
4. **Respect** — the Constitution never forgets it is built on Reality, Principles, and USCP
