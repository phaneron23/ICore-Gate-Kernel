# Atlas — Constitutional Sciences: UCE, UCC, UCM, UCL

*The four disciplines within the Constitution.*

---

## UCE — Universal Constitutional Epistemology

### Origin
The Constitution must first decide what can be known. Epistemology — the science of knowledge — is the first discipline because without knowing what is knowable, no other discipline can proceed.

### Nature
UCE is the **constitutional science of knowledge**. It defines the rules for truth claims: what counts as knowledge, how truth is verified, and how knowledge is derived within the system. It does not *produce* knowledge; it *governs* knowledge production.

### Structure
```
UCE governs:
├── Truth — what statements can be considered true
├── Verification — how truth is checked
├── Derivation — how new knowledge is derived from existing knowledge
└── Boundaries — what is unknowable within the system
```

### Culture
UCE is the constitution's **epistemic immune system**. It prevents the system from claiming to know things it cannot verify. In ICore, every knowledge claim must pass UCE's rules before it can be used in governance or derivation.

**Relationship to other sciences:** UCE feeds into UCC — calculus cannot reason about what epistemology has not first classified as knowable.

---

## UCC — Universal Constitutional Calculus

### Origin
Once UCE defines what can be known, UCC provides the **logic** for reasoning about it. Calculus — in the classical sense of a formal reasoning system — derives from epistemology because logic presupposes a theory of knowledge.

### Nature
UCC is the **constitutional science of reasoning**. It provides the formal rules for combining, deriving, and validating constitutional truths. Every inference in ICore must follow UCC's rules; every derivation must be logically sound.

### Structure
```
UCC governs:
├── Inference — how conclusions are drawn from premises
├── Consistency — how contradictions are detected and resolved
├── Composition — how truths are combined into larger truths
└── Validity — how arguments are checked for soundness
```

### Culture
UCC is the constitution's **logic engine**. It ensures that every derivation in the system is formally valid — not just plausible, but *provably correct* within the constitutional framework.

**Relationship to other sciences:** UCC derives from UCE and feeds into UCM — mathematics requires a logical substrate.

---

## UCM — Universal Constitutional Mathematics

### Origin
Mathematics provides the **formal structures** that make precise representation possible. It derives from UCC because mathematical structures must be logically consistent; they cannot contradict the reasoning system they build on.

### Nature
UCM is the **constitutional science of structure**. It provides the mathematical frameworks for representing constitutional entities, their relationships, and their transformations. In ICore, UCM is not abstract mathematics — it is *constitutional* mathematics, grounded in the Constitution's own logic.

### Structure
```
UCM provides:
├── Set Theory — how entities are grouped and related
├── Graph Theory — how the derivation graph is formalized
├── Category Theory — how transformations between entities are modeled
└── Information Theory — how constitutional knowledge is measured
```

### Culture
UCM is the constitution's **formal backbone**. It transforms intuitive constitutional relationships into precise mathematical structures that can be computed, verified, and compared.

**Relationship to other sciences:** UCM derives from UCC and feeds into UCL — a language requires mathematical structure to express meaning without loss.

---

## UCL — Universal Constitutional Language

### Origin
The final science: expression. After knowledge (UCE), reasoning (UCC), and structure (UCM), the Constitution needs a **language** — a canonical way to express everything it knows and reasons about. UCL derives from all three predecessor sciences because language requires truth (UCE), logic (UCC), and structure (UCM) to be meaningful.

### Nature
UCL is the **constitutional science of expression**. It is the single semantic substrate from which all representations are derived. Crucially, UCL separates *meaning* from *serialization*: meaning is constitutional; JSON-LD, CBOR-LD, and every other format are replaceable implementations.

### Structure
```
UCL expresses:
├── Semantics — what things mean (their constitutional significance)
├── Syntax — how meanings are structured (their formal shape)
├── Primitives — how basic concepts are represented
└── Composition — how complex meanings are built from simple ones
```

### Culture
UCL is the constitution's **lingua franca**. Every other layer — models, representations, visualizations, runtime — is expressed in UCL. Changing the serialization format (JSON-LD → CBOR-LD) does not change UCL; changing UCL changes everything.

**Relationship to other sciences:** UCL is the convergence point of all four sciences. It receives from UCM and feeds into UCRS — the reference system must be expressed in the canonical language.

---

## The Four Together

```
UCE (knowledge) → UCC (reasoning) → UCM (structure) → UCL (expression)
```

Each science builds on the previous one. Together, they form the Constitution's **intellectual infrastructure** — the disciplines that make constitutional governance possible.

| Science | Question | Output |
|---------|----------|--------|
| UCE | What can we know? | Knowledge rules |
| UCC | How do we reason? | Logic rules |
| UCM | How do we structure? | Mathematical frameworks |
| UCL | How do we express? | Canonical language |

---

*The four sciences are constitutional disciplines within USC. They are governed by the Constitution and govern everything above them.*
