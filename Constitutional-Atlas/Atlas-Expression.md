# Atlas — Constitutional Expression: UCRS & UCModels

*How the Constitution represents itself.*

---

## UCRS — Universal Constitutional Reference System

### Origin
After the Constitution (USC) and its four sciences, it needs to know **where everything is**. UCRS is the Constitution's self-representation — the map of its own space. It does not reference something external; it references USC itself.

### Nature
UCRS is the **constitutional reference system** — the canonical way to locate, identify, and navigate every entity within the constitution. It is the Constitution's answer to "where am I?" and "where is everything else?"

### Structure
```
UCRS provides:
├── Coordinates — unique positions in constitutional space
├── Navigation — paths between constitutional entities
├── Orientation — the derivation direction (downward)
└── Self-Reference — USC's own position in its own space
```

### Culture
UCRS is the constitution's **GPS**. Without it, constitutional entities exist but cannot be found. With it, every entity has a unique, verifiable position that any system can navigate to.

**Key insight:** UCRS is the Constitution's way of knowing itself. It is not an external observer looking in; it is the Constitution looking at itself.

---

## UCModels — Universal Canonical Models

### Origin
After the Constitution knows where things are (UCRS), it needs to know **what they are** in formal terms. UCModels provides the canonical models — the shared, verifiable representations of constitutional entities, their relationships, and their processes.

### Nature
UCModels is the **ontological backbone** of ICore. It defines the canonical forms that all downstream systems consume. Models in UCModels are not approximations; they are the *authoritative* representations that everything else must align with.

### Structure
```
UCModels provides:
├── Entity Models — what constitutional entities look like
├── Relationship Models — how entities connect
├── Process Models — how entities change (lifecycle)
└── Governance Models — how the constitution itself is governed
```

### Culture
UCModels is the constitution's **ontology**. It is the single source of truth for what constitutional entities are and how they relate. All downstream systems — representations, visualizations, runtime — consume UCModels.

**Dependency:** UCModels requires both UCL (to express meaning) and UCRS (to orient entities in constitutional space).

---

*UCRS and UCModels form the Constitution's self-awareness: knowing where things are and what they are.*
