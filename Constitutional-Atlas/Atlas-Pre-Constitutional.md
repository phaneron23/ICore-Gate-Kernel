# Atlas — Pre-Constitutional: Reality & Principles

*What exists before the constitution begins.*

---

## Reality

### Origin
Reality is the ground of everything — the raw, uninterpreted fact that something exists before we ask what it is or how it governs. In ICore, Reality is the **pre-constitutional substrate**: the world as it is, independent of any constitutional framework.

### Nature
Reality is **not** part of the constitution. It is what the constitution is *about*. The constitution does not create Reality; it governs how we *reason about* Reality. This distinction is critical: ICore does not claim to define what exists — it defines how to build trustworthy systems that *interact with* what exists.

### Structure
```
Reality
├── Physical — what exists in the material world
├── Informational — what exists as data, signals, patterns
├── Relational — how things connect independent of our models
└── Temporal — the passage of time, causation, sequence
```

### Culture
Reality is the constitution's **humbling ground**. It reminds ICore that no matter how elegant the framework, it must ultimately *work in the real world*. The adapter layer (UCA) is where the constitution meets Reality — and Reality always has the final say.

---

## Principles

### Origin
Between Reality and the Constitution lie Principles — the fundamental truths that bridge raw existence and governed structure. Principles are not constitutional rules (that is Constraint's job); they are the **insights** that make constitutional governance possible.

### Nature
Principles are **pre-constitutional truths** that the constitution is built on. They are not derived from the constitution; the constitution is derived from them. In ICore, Principles are the philosophical foundations that make the six primitives sensible.

### Structure
```
ICore Principles
├── Sovereignty — the system governs itself
├── Minimalism — the smallest sufficient foundation
├── Verifiability — every claim must be checkable
├── Derivation — everything traces to its source
├── Transparency — the system's logic is visible
└── Sovereignty-over-external — the constitution never depends on external systems
```

### Culture
Principles are the constitution's **conscience**. When governance decisions are made, Principles provide the ethical and philosophical framework. They are not rules to be checked against; they are *values* to be held.

### The Bridge
```
Reality → Principles → USCP → USC
         (bridge)      (atoms) (authority)
```

Principles bridge Reality and USCP. They translate raw existence into constitutional primitives. Without Principles, USCP would be arbitrary — six atoms chosen without reason. With Principles, USCP is inevitable — six atoms that *must* exist for any trustworthy system.

---

*Reality and Principles are pre-constitutional. They ground the constitution but are not governed by it. They are the soil from which the constitution grows.*
